# Generation model marks

These files are unmodified marks published by the named model creators. They are
used in Fanta Edit's generation catalog to identify the models and their makers.
The names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. A provider
mark is identified as such below; it is not presented as a separate logo for a
particular model version. Retrieved 2026-09-27.
